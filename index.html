Do we need to know which AI tool we want?

No. We begin with the business problem and current process. Tools come after the desired result, constraints, and risks are understood.

Will we need to replace our software?

Not by default. The first goal is to get more value from the systems you already have. Replacement should happen only when a current tool is a material constraint.

Is this just an AI training workshop?

No. Training is one possible play. Affinity also redesigns workflows, deploys tailored automations, improves revenue visibility, and measures the result.

Do you implement the recommendations?

Yes. Affinity can design, configure, test, and deploy a qualified workflow through a fixed-scope implementation sprint. Your team receives training, documentation, ownership, and a defined support period.

What happens during the free AI Time Audit?

We walk through how your day and week are spent, examine one or two recurring tasks, estimate the potential opportunity, and decide whether an implementation sprint—or a simpler solution—makes sense.
