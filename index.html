Do we need to know which AI tool we want?

No. We start with the business problem and how the work is done today. We choose tools only after we know the goal, limits, and risks.

Will we need to replace our software?

No. We first try to get more from the tools you already pay for. We recommend replacing software only if it is clearly getting in the way.

Is this just an AI training workshop?

No. Training may be part of the work. We can also fix workflows, build automations, improve lead follow-up and reporting, and help your team use the new process.

Do you implement the recommendations?

Yes. If we both agree on a project, Affinity can build, test, and launch the workflow. We also train your team, document it, and stay available during a set support period.

What happens during the free AI Time Audit?

After the assessment, qualified businesses can book a 30-minute call. We look at one or two recurring tasks, estimate the time involved, and decide whether a small project or a simpler fix makes sense.
